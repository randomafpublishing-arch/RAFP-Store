Digital Product Policy

Random AF Publishing products are digital products. Because digital products cannot be physically returned, purchases are generally final once delivered or accessed.

If you experience a genuine technical issue with a product, contact us so we can help determine the best solution.

Personal Use License

Your purchase is licensed for your own personal use unless the product specifically states otherwise.

Products may not be resold, redistributed, copied for others, repackaged, uploaded for public sharing, or presented as your own work.

Privacy & Your Content

Your personal entries, photos, captions, memories, and other content you create inside compatible RAFP interactive products belong to you.

When a product is designed for local device storage, your personal content stays on your own device unless you choose to export, back up, or move it yourself.

Compatibility

Product requirements can vary. Always review the product description before purchasing to confirm that the product is suitable for your device and intended use.

RAFP products are designed with mobile convenience in mind whenever possible.

Updates

Some products may receive improvements, fixes, or updated versions over time. Update availability, access, and eligibility may vary by product and will be explained when applicable.

Payment & Delivery

Random AF Publishing uses a secure third-party checkout provider for payment processing and digital delivery.

Pricing, payment confirmation, and delivery information will be shown during the purchase process.

Technical Support

If a product is not opening, loading, restoring, or working as described, contact RAFP with the product name and a short explanation of the issue.

Support: randomafpublishing@gmail.com

Misuse & Access

Access to a product may be restricted when there is evidence of unauthorized sharing, resale, redistribution, abuse, fraud, or other misuse.

Policy Changes

RAFP may update store policies as products, technology, or business needs change. The version shown on this website is the current store policy.
